Idle Hearts

Privacy Policy · Effective November 7, 2026

The short version

Idle Hearts does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (currencies, upgrades, completed dates and settings) is saved in a file inside the app on your iPhone. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it. You can also erase it any time from Settings → Reset progress.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks or analytics services. If future versions add optional in-app purchases, payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Children

Idle Hearts does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date above. Questions? See the support page.
