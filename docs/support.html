How do I play?

Tap the big heart to build Chemistry between the couple on screen. Fill the bar to complete the date, earn Love Notes, and hire your agency team so the sparks keep flying while you're away. Tap the ? button on the home screen for a full guide.

Is my progress saved?

Yes — automatically, every few seconds and whenever you leave the app. Progress is stored only on your device.

I found a bug / have an idea

Open an issue at github.com/aquabunnyx3/idle-hearts/issues and include your iPhone model and iOS version.
