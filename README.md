# Idle Hearts 💘

*Love. Laughter. Higher profits.*

A native iPhone idle/clicker romance game built with **SwiftUI + SpriteKit**. You run a chaotic
matchmaking agency: tap the heart to build Chemistry, complete dates, hire your team, and earn
Love Notes even while you're away.

## Requirements
- Xcode 16 or newer (Xcode 26 for App Store uploads) · iOS 17+ · iPhone, portrait

## Run it
1. Open `IdleHearts.xcodeproj`.
2. Target **IdleHearts** → Signing & Capabilities → choose your Team.
3. Pick an iPhone (simulator or device) and press ⌘R.

Tip: add `-demo` to the scheme's launch arguments (already present, unticked) to start in a mid-game
state that is never saved. `-tab matches|agency|story` and `-overlay welcome|complete|drama` open
specific screens — that's how the App Store screenshots are produced.

## Game design
| Currency | Earned by | Spent on |
|---|---|---|
| ❤️ Chemistry | Taps, employees (per second) | Tap Power, Critical Spark Chance |
| ✉️ Love Notes | Completing dates; every completed couple pays forever (also offline) | Hiring/levelling staff, departments, income upgrades |
| 🎭 Drama | 1.5% of taps, +2 per date | Cause Drama (big chemistry burst + story event) |
| 💎 Heart Gems | Dates, drama events | Recharging boosters, doubling offline earnings |

Dates cycle through six hand-built locations (café, hotel, festival, restaurant, rooftop, park), each
with a cast pairing and a commentator, then repeat as numbered encores with rising requirements.
Partners gain relationship levels that unlock character story chapters.

Progress is saved as JSON in Application Support every 10 seconds and when the app backgrounds.
